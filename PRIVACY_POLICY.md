# Privacy Policy for Kove Mirror

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026

Kove Mirror ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Kove Mirror is designed with privacy as a fundamental principle:
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No User Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics, tracking frameworks, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or telemetry trackers.
- **On-Device Processing:** All connection state, screen mirroring, and hardware handshake procedures are executed entirely on your local device and directly between your iPhone and your motorcycle's dashboard.

## 2. Device Permissions
Kove Mirror requests specific system permissions strictly to provide core motorcycle companion functions:
- **Bluetooth:** Used to discover, pair, and maintain communication with your motorcycle's TFT dashboard, synchronizing altitude, temperature, and handlebar inputs.
- **Camera:** Used strictly to scan motorcycle Wi-Fi configuration QR codes for automatic access point setup, and for optional ambient light proximity detection to dim the screen while riding. Camera frames are processed strictly in memory in real time and are never recorded or transmitted.
- **Location:** Used to read the active Wi-Fi SSID for connection confirmation, display your current position on the in-app map, and provide turn-by-turn navigation data to the dashboard display. Location data never leaves your device.
- **Local Network:** Used to stream video frames (H.264 NAL units) across the local Wi-Fi link directly to the motorcycle TFT display.

## 3. In-App Purchases
Optional voluntary tip jar purchases are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or process any payment or financial information.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Kove Mirror does not integrate with any external cloud databases or third-party advertising networks.
